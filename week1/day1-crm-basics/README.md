# Day 1 - CRM Basics

## 1. What is CRM?
[cite_start]CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system that helps companies manage customer interactions, sales, and business workflows to improve relationships and grow the business[cite: 5, 6, 20].

## 2. Why companies use Salesforce?
[cite_start]Companies use Salesforce because it provides a centralized platform to manage customer data, track sales opportunities, and automate complex business processes[cite: 34, 59].

## 3. Important Salesforce Terms
- [cite_start]**Account:** Represents a company or organization[cite: 38, 61].
- [cite_start]**Contact:** Represents an individual person associated with an account[cite: 38, 62].
- [cite_start]**Opportunity:** Represents a potential sales deal or business event[cite: 39, 63].
- [cite_start]**Lead:** Represents a potential customer who has shown interest but isn't a customer yet[cite: 43, 52].

## 4. Business Flow
**Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer**
[cite_start]This flow represents the journey of a potential interest (Lead) being converted into a real person (Contact) with a specific deal in progress (Opportunity), eventually becoming a paying client (Customer)[cite: 43, 72].
